Rules & Game Guide

🎮 Game Modes

vs Computer: Challenge an AI equipped with 3 intellect levels — Easy (random & playful), Medium (tactical blocks), and Master (unbeatable Minimax game theory).

2-Player PvP: Pass and play locally with a friend on the same screen.

✨ Rule Variants

Classic 3x3: The beloved standard. First player to connect 3 marks horizontally, vertically, or diagonally wins.

Fading Memory (Infinite Mode): Never tie again! Each player is allowed a maximum of 3 marks on the board. When you place your 4th mark, your oldest piece evaporates! The oldest piece pulses in warning so you can plan tactical traps.

⌨️ Keyboard Controls

Use keys 1 through 9 on your numeric keypad or top row to trigger the corresponding cell, or navigate using Arrow Keys and press Enter or Space.
